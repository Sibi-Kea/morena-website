Our story

Rooted in Lesotho. Focused on your brands.

Morena Sales and Merchandising is a proudly registered Lesotho distribution, sales and merchandising company. Headquartered in Maseru, we cover the entire country and bring more than 20 years of experience in this market.

Our team works across departments to support our partners’ products, meet forecasts and targets, and understand the needs of Lesotho’s customers and consumers.
