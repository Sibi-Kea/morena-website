Why Morena

A local team, connected to your market

Morena Sales and Merchandising is a proudly registered Lesotho distribution, sales and merchandising company. Headquartered in Maseru, we cover the entire country and bring more than 20 years of experience in this market.

  • Local market knowledge — a team that understands Lesotho’s customers and consumers.
  • Nationwide coverage — across informal, formal and specialised trading channels.
  • Partner focus — working towards forecasts, targets and sustainable growth.
