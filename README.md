# Morena Sales website

Morena company content imported into the original Nexora Bootstrap template. The original `css/custom.css` is preserved, including its colours, typography, gradients, cards and animations.

Run locally with `python -m http.server 8080`, then open http://localhost:8080.

Content and local image sources (retrieved 11 September 2026):
- https://morenasales.com/ — company name, Moving Brands tagline, official logo and Lesotho map.
- https://morenasales.com/about-us/ — company profile, 20+ years of market experience, nationwide coverage and market channels.
- https://morenasales.com/services/ — service overview image and capabilities.
- https://morenasales.com/contact-us/ — Maseru address, email and telephone numbers.
- https://morenasales.com/vacancies/ — the listed role closed in May 2021; the careers page directs visitors to enquire about current opportunities.

Nestle's official Lesotho page lists Morena (Sales & Merchandising) and names Richard Haswell as its contact. The website describes this as a published contact listing, without claiming exclusivity or a specific job title. No current vacancies, pricing or testimonials were verified. Template examples have been replaced with company capabilities and enquiry content. Original portfolio, blog and pricing URLs now present market coverage, careers and service enquiries within the template.

The contact form validates input and prepares a mailto draft. Visitors must send it through their email app; no backend or newsletter service is configured. The map shows the published address area, not a verified building pin.

Original template: Nexora by Harshad Mahadik, distributed by ThemeWagon. Original README described the template as MIT licensed. Original template attribution is retained here; the website footer displays Powered by Pop In Solutions.
